# Israeli Paid Ads

## Instructions

### Step 1: Choose Platform and Campaign Type

Select the right platform based on business type, audience, and campaign objective.

| Platform | Best For | Israeli Audience | Avg CPM (NIS) | Ad Formats |
|----------|----------|------------------|---------------|------------|
| Google Ads (Search) | High-intent traffic, lead gen | 98.17% search market share in Israel | N/A (CPC model) | Text ads, responsive search ads |
| Meta (Facebook) | B2C, community, local business | ~7.6M Israeli users | 15-40 | Image, video, carousel, collection |
| Instagram | Lifestyle, fashion, food, travel | ~5M Israeli users | 20-50 | Stories, reels, feed, shopping |
| LinkedIn | B2B, SaaS, enterprise, recruiting | ~3M Israeli professionals | 40-120 | Single image / document / video, Sponsored Messaging |
| TikTok | Gen Z, viral content, brand awareness | Growing Israeli user base (18-34) | 10-30 | In-feed video, branded effects |

### Step 2: Hebrew Keyword Research

Hebrew is morphologically rich: a single root produces dozens of inflections that keyword tools do not group automatically (verb conjugations, noun forms, construct state, with/without definite article, colloquial spellings). Missing one silently forfeits its search volume. Also check English/transliterated variants Israelis actually search.

### Step 3: Write Hebrew Ad Copy

- Use informal register (guf sheni): "אתה/את" not "אתם"
- Include prices WITH VAT - Israeli law requires all advertised prices to include 18% VAT
- Add local trust signals: "חברה ישראלית", "שירות בעברית", local phone prefix
- Always preview in RTL - mixed Hebrew/English/digit strings can reorder unexpectedly

### Step 4: Israeli Audience Targeting

- Military service gap: the "young professional" segment starts at 22-23 in Israel, not 18
- Shabbat scheduling: most Israeli consumers are inactive Friday 14:00 through Saturday evening - pause or reduce bids
- Hebrew calendar holidays shift yearly - pause campaigns on Yom Kippur
- Consent for ad targeting (Amendment 13): uploading a customer list, using it as a lookalike/Advantage+ seed, or pixel/CAPI remarketing all require explicit, informed, granular consent
- Contacting leads (Spam Law): prior opt-in required, message must carry "פרסומת" and a working opt-out, violation carries statutory damages up to ~1,000 NIS per message

### Step 5: Ad Regulations (Chok Haganat HaTzarchan)

- VAT-inclusive pricing is mandatory by law
- Sponsored content must display "פרסומת" or "תוכן ממומן"
- Influencer partnerships must be disclosed with #פרסומת, visible without clicking "more"
- Cancellation rights: standard distance-sale window is 14 days (timing varies by transaction type), but a new immigrant (oleh chadash), a person with a disability, or a person 65+ gets FOUR MONTHS instead - most agents and businesses omit this entirely

### Step 6: Set Up Conversion Measurement (before Step 7)

Install the tag/pixel + server-side Conversions API, deduplicate with a shared event ID, pass value + currency (ILS), decide the VAT basis, fire a test conversion before spending any budget.

### Step 7: Budget and Bidding, and VAT/ROAS

For a VAT-registered business (osek murshe), the 18% VAT on your ad invoice is reclaimable input VAT - it is NOT a real cost and must NOT go into your ROAS denominator. Use ex-VAT ad spend against ex-VAT (or gross-vs-gross, consistently) revenue. Only divide by 1.18 if you are an osek patur who cannot reclaim VAT.

Bundled `scripts/cpc_calculator.py` estimates campaign costs by vertical and budget, treating VAT correctly.

## Gotchas (common agent mistakes this skill exists to prevent)

- Generating ad copy with pre-VAT prices (breaches Consumer Protection Law)
- Dividing ad spend by 1.18 for a VAT-registered business when input VAT is reclaimable
- Running campaigns 24/7 through Shabbat and Yom Kippur
- Targeting only one Hebrew inflection of a keyword and missing the rest of the search volume
- Building "high income" or condition-based audiences for finance/insurance/legal/health advertisers - both Google and Meta restrict sensitive-category targeting
- Writing a flat "14-day cancellation right" and missing the four-month right for olim, people with disabilities, and 65+
